# Mehrunissa Owens

**Research · Education · Clinical care · AI evaluation**

I’m interested in how people and AI systems make decisions when information is incomplete or changes—and what happens when one small decision sends the rest of a process in the wrong direction.

My background spans experimental psychology, clinical and behavioral research, research data quality, science education, and paramedicine. Across those fields, I have designed research materials and assessments, worked through discrepancies, taught people with different ways of learning, and brought together changing information to decide what needs attention.

## What I bring

- **Research and data quality:** Developing study materials, case-report forms, and procedures; reviewing discrepancies; and coordinating with clinical, programming, statistical, and research teams.
- **Education:** Designing lessons and assessments, adapting explanations, and giving feedback that helps learners try again.
- **Clinical care:** Assessing changing conditions, checking protocols, and communicating findings to other clinicians.
- **Independent AI evaluation:** Examining AI responses for missing context, discrepancies, and unsupported conclusions; tracing errors back through an interaction; and testing revised instructions. This is independent work, separate from formal employment.

## What I’m exploring

I want to help develop AI that can reason in systems: recognize relationships and feedback loops, reassess an assumption when new information appears, and explain where a decision went off course. I am interested in human–AI collaboration, model evaluation, and ways to turn careful observation into useful feedback.
